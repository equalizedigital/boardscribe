# Market Research

---

## The Opportunity

There are effectively **zero direct competitors** on WordPress.org. The two existing plugins have fewer than 100 active installs combined, and one has already discontinued its premium tier. No meeting minutes plugin exists on CodeCanyon either.

### Addressable Market (US Only)

| Segment | Total Orgs | WordPress Estimate |
|---|---|---|
| HOAs | 373,000 | 55,000–93,000 |
| School districts | 13,300 | 2,000–3,300 |
| Local government | 77,500 | 11,000–19,000 |
| Nonprofits (board-governed) | ~500,000 | 75,000–125,000 |

Capturing just 0.5% at $149/year = ~$75K ARR. 1% = ~$150K ARR.

---

## Pricing

SaaS alternatives (BoardBreeze, ClerkMinutes) charge **$360–$2,988/year**. A WordPress plugin at $99–$199/year represents a 65–80% cost reduction — a compelling value proposition for budget-constrained HOAs, school boards, and nonprofits.

Benchmarked against comparable niche B2B plugins (Complianz, PublishPress, Document Library Pro):

| Tier | Price | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Single site | $79–$99/year | Individual orgs |
| 5 sites | $149–$199/year | Management companies |
| Unlimited | $299–$399/year | HOA firms, district offices |

---

## Freemium-Lite Model

**Recommendation: Freemium-lite** — a genuinely useful free version on WordPress.org, with premium sold via our own website for governance-critical features.

**Why not fully premium only:** No existing audience in this niche yet. WordPress.org discovery provides organic installs, reviews, and legitimacy with government/nonprofit IT buyers who vet plugins there before recommending them to administrators.

**Why not full freemium:** Freemium conversion rates are 1–5% industry-wide and require volume to work. This niche will not drive enough free installs fast enough for pure freemium to pay off. The buyers are rational B2B decision-makers who will purchase when the feature justifies it.

### Free vs. Premium Feature Split

**Free (WordPress.org)**
- Core CPT + shortcode with default table display
- Basic year filtering and pagination
- ACF integration

**Premium (sold via our website)**
- Gutenberg block with inspector controls
- Additional display templates
- Export to CSV/PDF
- Email notifications and RSS feed
- iCal/calendar feed
- Bulk CSV import
- Approval workflow and quorum tracking
- Action item tracking
- Document versioning
- WCAG accessibility compliance reporting
- Deeper Accessibility Checker Pro integration

## Distribution

Premium licenses sold directly via our own website using **Easy Digital Downloads (EDD)** with the Software Licensing extension.

**Why EDD direct:**
- Full control over pricing, discounts, and customer relationships
- No ongoing revenue share to a third party
- Software Licensing extension handles license keys, activation limits, and auto-update delivery
- Better economics at scale than any marketplace or managed platform
- Ownership of customer data and purchase history

**Supporting channels:**
- **Free version on WordPress.org** — required for organic discovery and credibility; government/nonprofit IT buyers vet plugins here before recommending purchases
- **Direct outreach** to HOA management companies and nonprofit WordPress agencies — these firms manage dozens of sites each, making a single sale multiply across a multi-site license
- **Content marketing** targeting compliance searches — "how to publish HOA meeting minutes on WordPress," "school board public records WordPress" — low competition, high buyer intent
